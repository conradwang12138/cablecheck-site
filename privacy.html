CableCheck privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026.

Local records

CableCheck is provided by Ziheng Wang and reads USB-C connection information on your Mac. Saved records include your cable name and notes, check time, Mac model and system version, relevant port paths, device names and necessary identity fields, readings, sources and parser versions. The app has no account, advertising, analytics SDK or automatic upload service.

Diagnostics and exports

Inspection creates bounded local diagnostics to trace reading status and connection changes. PDF and JSON exports are written only to a location you choose; they are not automatically sent to the developer. Reports may contain device names and connection evidence. Inspect them before sharing. Cloud folders and sharing services you choose process files under their own policies.

Storage and deletion

Cable records stay in the app sandbox. You can delete a cable and its checks; export first if you want a copy. System backups and exported copies are managed through your chosen system settings and services.

Purchases

The app is a paid App Store download. Apple handles purchases and payment. There is no payment-card form, subscription or in-app purchase in the app.

Support

If you email support, the recipient and email provider process your sender address, message and optional screenshots or diagnostics to respond and investigate. Reports are optional. Contact wzh925704928@gmail.com to request deletion of information you sent.

Message bodies, attachments and support copies are deleted within 90 days after resolution; earlier deletion may be requested. Email-provider backups follow their policies. This does not affect your local cable records.

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages without advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, whether or not visitors are signed in. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement.
