CableCheck 支持

在 Mac 上查看 USB-C 连接，为实体线材保存记录、翻转复测并导出报告。

开始检查

连接设备或充电器，在“当前检查”中选择对应端口。大号读数表示当前设备链路或所选供电上限；线材标称规格单独显示。所选供电上限不是即时功率。

保存与比较

点击“保存记录”，新建或选择档案并确认实体线材归属。翻转时保持同一个 Mac 端口和对端设备；连接存储设备时先安全推出。历史中勾选两条记录，确认条件后比较并导出 PDF 或 JSON。

没有读到线材规格

Mac 不一定提供线材身份。仍可查看可用的当前连接信息；未知不代表坏线或低规格。若显示上次结果，刷新并等待连接稳定后再保存。

CableCheck support

Inspect USB-C connections on Mac, keep records for physical cables, retest a flipped plug and export reports.

Start a check

Connect a device or charger and select its port in Inspect connection. Prominent readings show the current device link or selected power limit. Rated cable specifications appear separately. The selected power ceiling is not instantaneous power.

Save and compare

Choose Save record, create or select a cable record and confirm its physical assignment. Keep the Mac port and far-end device unchanged during a flip test; safely eject storage before unplugging. Select two history records, confirm their conditions, then compare and export PDF or JSON.

Cable specifications unavailable

Your Mac may not expose cable identity. Available connection readings remain useful; unknown does not establish damage or a low specification. If the app shows an older result, refresh and wait for a stable connection before saving.
